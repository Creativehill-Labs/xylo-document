# XYLO Launchpad

The Xylo Launchpad is a blockchain-powered platform for investing in tokenized real-world assets (RWAs). It is designed to help users easily discover, analyze, and subscribe to a wide range of RWA opportunities—anytime, anywhere, through web or mobile.

Key Features:&#x20;

* Seamless access via Web3 wallets or Xylo accounts
* Strong security using MPC and Multi-Sig
* Transparent smart contract-based subscription processes
* Intuitive and user-friendly interface

### 1. Access & Login

Login Options:

* Web3 Wallet - Supports MetaMask, Trust Wallet, and other external wallets.
* Xylo Account - Secure login via ID/password with 2FA (OTP, biometric authentication).

Security:

* 2-Factor Authentication (2FA)
* Multi-layer asset protection using MPC & Multi-Sig

***

### 2. Explore Investment Opportunities

Users can explore a curated list of RWA products directly on the platform.

Search Filters:

* Subscription Status: Ongoing, Closing Soon, Completed
* Asset Type: Casino rights, Real Estate, U.S. Stocks, Green Energy, etc.
* Expected Yield (%), Risk Level, Duration, Minimum Investment

UX Features:

* Real-time updates on subscription status and participation rates
* Visual display of token price changes and remaining availability

***

### 3. RWA Product Details

Each RWA product includes detailed information for informed decision-making.

Information Provided:

* Asset Overview: Description, legal structure, revenue model
* Token Details: Supply, subscription terms, reward structure
* Risk Assessment: Expert reports and valuation documents
* Smart Contract Info: On-chain contract addresses for transparency

***

### 4. XUSD-Based Subscription

Investments are made using XUSD, Xylo’s stablecoin.

Subscription Process:

* Input the desired amount of XUSD
* Participate via either First-Come, First-Served or Lottery-Based Allocation
* Smart contracts handle fund processing and allocation automatically

Allocation Modes:

* FCFS: Immediate confirmation of allocation
* Lottery: Users lock funds for a period and are selected randomly. Refunds processed for unsuccessful entries.

***

### 5. Portfolio Management

After subscription, users can manage their assets through My Portfolio.

Portfolio Features:

* Track all RWA token holdings and current valuations
* Monitor real-time investment returns
* Review pending or in-progress subscriptions

Asset Management Tools:

* Transfer RWA tokens to public blockchains
* Check tradability on CEXs/DEXs
* Convert RWA tokens back to XUSD or XLT
