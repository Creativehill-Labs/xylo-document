# MPC & Security

## 1. Overview

Your digital assets are more than just data—they represent your future. At Xylo, we recognize this responsibility. Through our RWA tokenization platform and launchpad, we enable access to real-world value while prioritizing wallet security and asset integrity.

To achieve this, we deploy industry-leading technologies including:

* MPC (Multi-Party Computation)
* Multi-Signature (Multi-Sig) verification
* Decentralized asset custody

These features ensure that your assets remain protected and verifiable at every stage.

***

## 2. MPC-Powered Wallet Security

Multi-Party Computation (MPC) is a next-generation cryptographic approach that splits a user's private key into multiple fragments, preventing any single point of compromise.

Advantages:

* No Single Point of Failure: The private key is never stored in one location—making it extremely difficult to hack.
* Tamper-Proof Access: Unless all key fragments are compromised simultaneously, unauthorized access is virtually impossible.
* Secure Recovery & Mobility: Even during wallet recovery or migration, security is preserved, and the user experience remains seamless.

## 3. Multi-Signature Control for Operational Transparency

Multi-Sig (Multi-Signature) technology requires approvals from multiple parties before any asset can be moved or withdrawn—adding a powerful layer of operational security.

Use Cases:

* Withdrawal of platform-held assets
* Management of pooled investor funds
* Allocation of capital through Launchpad projects

Benefits of Multi-Sig:

* Accountability: Prevents unilateral asset movements by any single operator.
* Internal Governance: Establishes a trusted, auditable process.
* Investor Confidence: All asset transactions are logged and verifiable.

***

## 4. USDT-Based Reserve & Distributed Custody Strategy

In addition to being backed 100% by real-world assets, a portion of investor funds is preserved in stable assets such as USDT to maintain liquidity and protect against market volatility.

Core Components:

* Stable Asset Reserve: A fixed percentage of investor funds is held in USDT or equivalent.
* Third-Party Custody: Funds are distributed across institutional-grade custodians to minimize internal risks.
* Real-Time Audits: Transparency is ensured via independent audits and structured reporting.

Expected Outcomes:

* Market Hedge: Reduces exposure to volatile market swings.
* Platform Trust: Reinforces the system’s ability to preserve investor capital.
* Built-in Investor Protection: Delivers a robust framework for long-term stability.
