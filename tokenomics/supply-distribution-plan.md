# Supply Distribution Plan

60% of XLT’s total circulating supply is stored across eight designated supply wallets.

* Wallet #1 (12%) is the only wallet unlocked for exchange listings (initially across five exchanges).
* Wallets #2–8 remain locked and are released gradually based on predefined market conditions.

Release Conditions:

* The circulating supply on exchanges drops below 6%, **or**
* The market price of XLT rises above a predetermined target level.

This mechanism helps prevent oversupply, encourages organic value growth based on real demand, and strengthens investor confidence by incentivizing long-term holding.
