# XLT Overview

XLT is the foundational token of the Xylo ecosystem, built to support the digital transformation of real-world assets. More than just a utility token, XLT serves as a store of value, liquidity balancer, and a scarcity-enhancing asset through its deflationary design.

Design Principles:

✅  **Controlled Initial Supply:** Prevents over-inflation and ensures price stability at launch.

✅  **Ongoing Burn Mechanism:** XLT is continuously burned through usage, increasing scarcity.

✅  **Natural Demand Growth:** Tied directly to demand from RWA subscriptions, reinforcing organic token value.

✅ **Long-Term Incentives:** Lock-up periods and staged token releases reward long-term holders and ecosystem supporters.

XLT is not designed for short-term speculation, but as a long-term growth asset linked to the fundamentals of real-world asset investment.
