# Token Economic Structure

## Token Economy Appreciation Model

### Assumptions

\#1. Ongoing demand from Launchpad RWA subscriptions

\#2. Gradual accumulation of burned tokens

\#3. Circulating supply remains fixed or grows slowly

\#4. Platform and user demand continue to increase

***

### **Price Logic Concept**

$$
XLT Price ≈ Platform Demand / Circulating Supply
$$

More users join and invest → More XLT is burned  → Circulating supply decreases + Demand increases \
→ Price rises naturally over time

***

### Example Scenario

* Total RWA subscription volume: $10,000,000
* XLT market price: $0.01 ⇒ 1,000,000,000 XLT burned

**Result:** 10% of the total supply permanently burned, significantly increasing market scarcity and potential market cap.
