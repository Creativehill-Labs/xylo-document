# Real Asset Valuation

## Why Asset Validation Matters

Tokenizing real-world assets (RWAs) unlocks liquidity—but only if the underlying asset is legal, accurately valued, and verifiable. Without trust in the real asset, tokenized versions lose credibility.

XYLO applies a rigorous evaluation process built on **3 key principles:**

* **Legality:** Legal ownership and rights verification 
* **Objectivity:** Third-party valuation and appraisal 
* **Transparency:** Full disclosure of all documentation and audit trails

***

## Full Verification Workflow

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Asset Registration Request

Asset owner submits documents (e.g. title deed, appraisal report, past transactions)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Documentation & Preliminary Review

Classification by asset type (e.g. real estate, infrastructure, fixed-income)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Legal Review (Jurisdictional Compliance)

Collaboration with local law firms to verify:

* Ownership legitimacy
* Transferability / lien status
* Foreign investor eligibility
* Legality of tokenization and rights transfer

Legal opinions are shared with investors as part of the disclosure package.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Valuation & Due Diligence

Conducted by partnered appraisal and credit rating firms

* Review of financials, revenue potential, and risk profile
* Market-based valuation models
* Independent audit trail and documentation

Based on IFRS or applicable GAAP standards
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Accounting & Financial Review

Partner accounting firms assess:

* Owner’s balance sheet and debt
* Projected cash flow
* Liquidity and income potential

Reports included in investor data packs before token issuance
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Risk Committee Review & Final Approval

Internal review of:

* Legal eligibility
* Economic viability
* Impact on platform liquidity
* Redemption/cash-out feasibility post-tokenization

Upon approval, token minting is initiated.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Documentation & Investor Disclosure

* Summary of legal review
* Appraisal methodology & outcome
* Financial audit reports
* Credit ratings or risk scores (if applicable)

All documents can be embedded on-chain via NFT or IPFS to ensure immutability and open access.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
