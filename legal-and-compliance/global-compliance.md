Our platform enables the tokenization of real-world assets (RWA), allowing global investors to fund them through blockchain infrastructure, while offering local currency liquidity to asset owners. This model enhances transparency, efficiency, and accessibility within the RWA market and establishes strong alignment with global financial regulations and compliance standards.

## 2. Detailed Breakdown by Phase

### **2-1. Asset Onboarding & Due Diligence**

* Legal ownership, asset valuation, and on-site inspection are verified
* Smart contracts define distribution rights and payout conditions

### **2-2. Blockchain-based Fundraising**

* RWA offerings are launched on the platform using XUSD or USDT
* Smart contracts automatically manage subscriptions and aggregate funds
* All transactions are transparently recorded on-chain

### **2-3. AML/CFT Compliance Framework**

**🔒 Know-Your-Customer (KYC)**

* Mandatory for both investors and asset owners
* Data collected includes:
  * Government-issued ID or passport
  * Proof of address
  * Source of funds & income verification

**🔐 AML Risk Scoring & Screening**

* Performed via third-party AML API or SaaS solutions
* Real-time checks for PEPs, sanctions lists, and transaction anomalies

### **2-4. Legal Local Currency Payouts**

**💱 FX Conversion & Cross-border Transfer**

* Funds are received in USDT or XUSD
* Platform converts funds into local currency in compliance with FX regulations
* Partners include registered FX brokers and remittance providers
* Capital movement permissions and tax filings are handled as needed

**🏦 Payout Execution**

* Funds are transferred to verified local bank accounts of asset owners
* Only contract-authorized payouts are processed
* Integrated with accounting systems for real-time record keeping

**📁 Legal Documentation**

* The following documents are auto-generated and archived:
  * RWA tokenization contract
  * Payout approval & KYC certificate
  * FX rate history and transaction proof

**🏛️ Regulatory Reporting**

* Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) filed with local FIUs if required
* Quarterly or semi-annual structured reports prepared for compliance
* External audits available via third-party accounting firms

**🔐 Security & Custody**

* Asset tokens and funds are stored in segregated MPC-based wallets
* Multi-sig approvals are required for all asset disbursements
* Blockchain hashes linked to all payouts ensure tamper-proof history
