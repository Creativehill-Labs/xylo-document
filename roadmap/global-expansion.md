# Global Expansion

XYLO was launched with a clear mission: to transform real-world assets (RWAs) into blockchain-based investment products. By connecting asset owners with global investors, XYLO is reshaping how value is stored, transferred, and monetized in the digital age. Following a successful initial launch, we are expanding our focus to global markets through strategic partnerships, crypto alliances, and a complementary ecosystem for RWA token adoption.

## 1. International Institutional Partnerships

### 1-1. Global Legal, Audit & Valuation Networks

We’re forming strategic alliances with international law firms, appraisal institutions, and accounting firms to standardize asset evaluation and build investor confidence.

* Unified RWA due diligence standards across jurisdictions
* Local partnerships with banks and investment firms
* Implementation of joint trust or insurance-backed custody models

***

## 2. Strategic Alliances with Major Crypto Entities

### 2-1. Coin Swap Partnerships

We aim to sign bilateral swap agreements with major crypto projects.\
Example: XLT ↔ BNB, MATIC, ETH, SOL

**Benefits:**

* Mutual token holdings to align interests and diversify risk
* Joint marketing and cross-community growth
* Locked swap terms to ensure long-term alignment

### 2-2. Co-Ecosystem Development

* Co-launch of RWA offerings via shared launchpads
* Interoperable token utility (e.g., stake XLT on partner platforms, collateralized DeFi loans)
* NFT-based co-management of tokenized RWAs

***

## 3. Risk-Diversified Asset Management

### 3-1. Value Preservation via Coin Swaps

To mitigate volatility, a portion of XLT reserves will be converted into ETH, BTC, and USDT.

* Promotes project stability and cross-asset credibility
* Aligns Xylo with leading Web3 infrastructures

### 3-2. Reserve Assets & Third-Party Custody

Key reserves will be held in USDT and U.S. Treasuries, managed by third-party custodians.

* Secure storage with MPC and Multi-Sig architecture
* Independent audits to prevent unauthorized outflows

***

## 4. Governance & Global DAO Integration

### 4-1. DAO Formation with XLT Governance

We will launch a decentralized governance system powered by XLT holders.\
Voting topics **include:**

* RWA listing approvals
* Partner onboarding
* Reserve management models

### 4-2. Expert Advisory Network

We will onboard global RWA experts who review new listings and provide on-chain due diligence reports prior to DAO voting.

***
