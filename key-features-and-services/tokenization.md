# Tokenization

XYLO’s RWA tokenization technology enables the digital transformation of real-world assets—such as real estate, bonds, fine art, and commodities—into blockchain-based tokens. This process helps resolve inefficiencies in traditional financial markets and facilitates easier, faster global access to tangible asset investments.&#x20;

Our tokenization process combines private (internal) and public (external) blockchains to ensure secure, transparent, and scalable asset lifecycle management across **5 key phases**:

### 1. Asset Analysis and Valuation

* Certified appraisers and auditors assess the asset’s value through our internal blockchain.
* Key metrics include market value, ownership validation, legal compliance, profitability, and risk analysis.
* All valuation data is immutably recorded on the internal blockchain for transparency and auditability.

### 2. Subscription and Allocation (via XYLO Launchpad)

* Investors can subscribe to RWA offerings through the XYLO Launchpad.
* The platform allows issuers to define investment amounts, eligibility criteria, and allocation rules.
* Smart contracts ensure fair, automated allocation with full traceability.

### 3. Token Issuance (on Internal Blockchain)

* Once subscribed, the asset is tokenized and issued on the internal blockchain.
* Tokens are pegged 1:1 to the underlying asset and governed by smart contracts that automate ownership, dividend distribution, and legal compliance.
* Issuance details are securely logged and verifiable on-chain.

### 4. Secondary Trading (on External Blockchain)

* Tokens are transferred to external blockchains to enable trading on CEXs, DEXs, and DeFi platforms.
* These tokens can be deposited into liquidity pools, used as collateral, or staked for yield.
* Oracle systems ensure seamless synchronization between on-chain data and traditional asset metadata.

### 5. Monitoring and Lifecycle Management

* All transactional data is tracked and logged on the internal blockchain.
* A real-time dashboard enables regulators, investors, and enterprises to monitor asset performance across its full lifecycle.
* This ensures continuous compliance and operational visibility.
