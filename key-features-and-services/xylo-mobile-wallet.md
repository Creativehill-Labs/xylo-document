# XYLO Mobile Wallet

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Frame 1000008396 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 1. Overview

The Xylo Mobile App is a secure, intuitive gateway for managing digital assets, participating in RWA investments, and accessing blockchain-based financial services. It removes the complexity of traditional crypto wallets and is optimized for both new and experienced users.

***

## 2. Key Features

### 2-1. Simple Access, No Seed Phrase

* Login with ID and password—no need for complicated seed phrases.
* Designed to lower the barrier for non-technical users.
* Solves usability issues found in tools like MetaMask.

### 2-2. Advanced Security Features

* Biometric login (fingerprint/face) and 2FA authentication for enhanced protection.
* Server-based wallet infrastructure provides enterprise-grade security.
* Backup and recovery options ensure asset safety.

### 2-3. Integrated Asset Management & Swaps

* Easily convert XLT to XUSD to access RWA investments.
* Swap between major cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, USDT) instantly.
* Supports flexible portfolio management.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Frame 1000004602.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 2-4. Direct RWA Launchpad Access

* Participate in RWA subscriptions directly via the app.
* Invest in real-world assets faster than in traditional finance.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Frame 1000008435 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 2-5. Real-Time Portfolio Dashboard

* View your balances, transaction history, and investment progress at a glance.
* Track XLT, XUSD, and other crypto assets in a unified interface.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Frame 1000008436 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 2-6. Secure Server-Based Wallet

* Uses a server-side wallet model instead of traditional private key management.
* Integrates Multi-Sig technology and automated backups.
* Minimizes risks of hacks or key loss.

### 2-7. Casino RWA Integration

* For casino-related RWA investors, the app enables token-to-chip conversion at partnered venues.
* Seamlessly bridge blockchain assets with real-world utility in offline environments.
