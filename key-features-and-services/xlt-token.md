## 1. Introduction to XLT

XLT is the native governance and utility token of the Xylo ecosystem, designed to facilitate participation in RWA (Real World Asset) investments. While it can be freely traded on multiple exchanges, its core purpose lies in enabling early access to high-quality RWA offerings via the Xylo Launchpad.

***

## 2. Key Features of XLT

### 2-1. Supply & Exchangeability

* **Total Supply:** 1 billion XLT (1,000,000,000 XLT)
* **Availability:** Tradable on major CEXs and DEXs. (See detailed [tokenomics](../tokenomics/xlt-overview.md) for distribution strategy.)

### 2-2. Launchpad Access

* XLT holders gain exclusive access to the Xylo RWA Launchpad.
* Early participants can invest in high-potential RWA assets before they reach public markets.
* Asset issuers are required to pay listing and tokenization fees in XLT.

### 2-3. XLT-to-XUSD Conversion for Investment

* To invest in RWA offerings, users must convert XLT into XUSD, Xylo’s stablecoin.
* All Launchpad subscriptions are conducted exclusively in XUSD.

### 2-4. Deflationary Burn Mechanism

* XLT used for XUSD conversion is permanently burned, reducing total supply.
* This scarcity-driven model is designed to increase long-term token value.
* As more RWA projects launch, continuous burning enhances token deflation.

### 2-5. Stability & Liquidity Management

* All XUSD tokens are backed 1:1 with reserves in USDT and USD/Treasury securities.
* This dual reserve model ensures XUSD maintains its value and earns user trust.

## 4. Advantages of the XLT Ecosystem

### 4-1. Deflationary Tokenomics

* Every XUSD minted leads to XLT being burned, driving scarcity and potential value growth.

### 4-2. Exclusive Investment Opportunities

* Only XLT holders can participate in the initial stages of RWA fundraising.
* Early access improves the likelihood of high returns.

### 4-3. Liquidity & Stability

* XUSD maintains price stability through full reserves, insulating users from market volatility.

### 4-4. Long-Term Value Proposition

* As participation in RWA investments grows, demand for XLT increases.
* The continuous burn mechanism reduces supply, potentially boosting long-term value.
