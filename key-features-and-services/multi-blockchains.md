# Multi Blockchains

Xylo leverages a hybrid blockchain model—combining the speed and control of a private chain with the global accessibility of public blockchains—to optimize performance and security throughout the RWA lifecycle.

### 1. Key Benefits of the Multi-Chain Model

🔹  <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">Internal Blockchain Advantages</mark>&#x20;

* **High-speed data recording:** Efficiently stores all asset-related data, including valuations, issuance, custody, and transactions.
* **Enhanced security:** Sensitive data remains protected within a permissioned network.
* **Tailored solutions:** Optimized for institutional investors and enterprise-grade compliance.

🔹  <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">External Blockchain Advantages</mark>&#x20;

* **Global liquidity:** Enables trading across various public chains and marketplaces.
* **Web3 & DeFi integration:** RWA tokens can interact with lending platforms, liquidity pools, and staking protocols.
* **Retail & institutional participation:** Open access through CEXs and DEXs invites a wider investor base.

***

### 2. Unified Blockchain Elements for Optimal Tokenization

* **Transparency:** Internal records are immutable, and external trades are verifiable. &#x20;
* **Security:** Uses MPC, multi-sig, and smart contracts to ensure safe token transfers. &#x20;
* **Liquidity:** Integrated with major DeFi protocols and exchanges for maximum utility. 
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Supports KYC/AML checks to meet local and international standards.



To transfer RWA tokens issued on the internal blockchain to external blockchain environments—such as CEXs, DEXs, and DeFi protocols—a series of technical processes must be followed. This procedure places critical importance on three factors: asset security, data integrity, and regulatory compliance.
