# Liquidity Provision

One of the central goals of Xylo is to bring blockchain-based liquidity into the world of traditional real assets. By tapping into the decentralized finance (DeFi) ecosystem, we solve the issue of illiquidity that has long plagued real-world investments.

&#x20;<mark style="background-color:green;">Key benefits:</mark>&#x20;

**Immediate Liquidity:** Tokenized assets can be traded quickly, making markets more accessible.

**Increased Inclusion:** Fractional ownership allows anyone to invest in assets like real estate or bonds.

**Reduced Costs:** Automated transactions and minimal intermediaries lower overall investment costs.

**Proven Transparency:** Blockchain ensures a secure, immutable record of all transactions.

***

### 1. Token Creation and Trading

* Physical assets are registered and tokenized on the blockchain
* Investors can buy and sell these tokens seamlessly within a decentralized environment.
* Ownership and transaction history are securely maintained on-chain.

### 2. DeFi-Powered Liquidity

* RWA tokens can be used as collateral within DeFi lending platforms.
* Investors can deposit assets into liquidity pools or stake them to earn returns.
* Both retail and institutional participants can access DeFi as a gateway to traditional asset investment.

### 3. Integration with Traditional Finance

* We work with established financial institutions to ensure full regulatory compliance.
* KYC/AML verification and legally structured smart contracts provide trust and legitimacy.
* Real-time data feeds linked to underlying assets improve transparency and reduce risk.
